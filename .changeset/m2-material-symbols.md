Material Symbols 2.975 (D5, Apache 2.0). `@kyralabs/m3e-tokens` ships the Rounded (default, D12), Outlined and Sharp variable fonts with their `.codepoints` maps and `material-symbols*.css`. `@kyralabs/m3e-icons` adds `subsetIcons()` and the `m3e-icons subset` CLI: keep only the named icons (ligature and code point), optionally pin or narrow FILL/wght/GRAD/opsz, and write a woff2 plus a drop-in `@font-face` — e.g. 4 icons with every axis ≈ 5 KB instead of 5.3 MB.
