Canonical layouts (M7, COMPONENT_SPEC §16a; Compose `ListDetailPaneScaffold`, `SupportingPaneScaffold`, `ThreePaneScaffold` and their navigators, plus the m3.material.io feed): list-detail and supporting-pane layouts that show one pane below 840px of their own width, two from 840px and three (with an extra pane) from 1600px — m3.material.io's Breakpoints; `pane-rule="compose"` / `paneRule="compose"` follows Compose's `calculatePaneScaffoldDirective`, three panes from 1200px — sized by a container query on `.m3e-layout`, so they adapt inside a navigation suite's content area. Navigation between panes follows Compose's navigator (`navigateTo`, `navigateBack` with the four back behaviors, history awareness), moves panes on Compose's pane motion (spring 0.8 / 380, the default enter / exit transitions, interruptible) and moves focus to the new destination and back. A pane expansion drag handle (`drag-handle` / `hasDragHandle`) splits two panes and settles on anchors. New `components/pane-scaffold-defaults.css` tokens (partition spacer, default pane sizes, pane motion spring, feed margins / gap / column width), m3e-css `layout.css` (`.m3e-layout`, `.m3e-pane-scaffold` with `--list-detail`, `--supporting-pane`, `--compose`, `__pane--primary|secondary|tertiary`, `__drag-handle`; `.m3e-feed` with `__item--wide`) and `vertical-drag-handle.css` (`.m3e-vertical-drag-handle`), m3e-motion `three-pane-scaffold` ports (`calculateThreePaneScaffoldValue`, `ThreePaneScaffoldNavigator`, `threePaneScaffoldDirective`, `threePaneScaffoldLayout`, `calculatePaneMotion`, `paneTransitions`, `ThreePaneScaffoldAnimation`, pane expansion anchors, `ListDetailPaneScaffoldRole`, `SupportingPaneScaffoldRole`), m3e-elements `<m3e-list-detail-layout>`, `<m3e-supporting-pane-layout>` and the `pane-scaffold-layout.js` (PaneScaffoldLayout), `pane-motion.js` and `pane-expansion.js` entries, and m3e-react `<ListDetailPaneScaffold>`, `<SupportingPaneScaffold>`, `<FeedLayout>`, `useListDetailPaneScaffoldNavigator`, `useSupportingPaneScaffoldNavigator`.
